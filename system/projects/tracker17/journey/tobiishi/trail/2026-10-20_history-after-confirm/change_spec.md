# Change Spec — Back after confirming a move to `cancelled` shows the saved status

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Category:** bug
**Behaviour:** changing
**Request:** Going Back in the browser after confirming a move to `cancelled` must not show the old confirmation again. Today it does: the browser keeps the confirmation page in its history cache, shows it on Back, and the strip with its confirming control comes back to life. The previous change tried `Clear-Site-Data: "cache"` to push the page out of that cache, and it was measured not to evict the page from the pinned browser, so `tests/e2e/test_moves.py::test_real_history_traversal_revalidates_the_confirmation` still fails (`1 failed, 56 passed` in the browser folder). Find a way to meet that case that is not that header, still with no page script on the pages (ADR-0003), and make the case pass on the real browser. The confirmation must still revalidate against the saved status: Back after confirming shows the saved-status refusal and no strip, a second tab's successful ordinary move leaves no usable old confirmation, and a reload, Back and Forward before confirming save nothing.

Then do what the previous change deferred behind it: review the change's file fence and the assertions it retained in the browser tests, so nothing outside the pages and routes that offer or confirm moves was touched and no assertion is kept that no longer checks anything. And record the history mechanism that gets settled on, as a record owed (an ADR or an SDD line), because no architecture record names either response header `web/routes.py` sends today (`Cache-Control: no-store` and `Clear-Site-Data: "cache"`).

Sizing decision: the acceptance boundary is the station's reached-when 5 — `pytest products/tracker17/tests/e2e/` exits 0 where layer 5 runs — whose one unmet obligation is the Back-after-confirm case (SDD § 4 EX-6, UXD UX-I2, change_spec CP-326/CP-331/CP-337 of the previous change). The mechanism, the fence review and the record owed are one capability's acceptance obligations, kept together; the station's other reached-when items already hold and are not reopened.

Must not be weakened: the workflow rule stays written once in `domain/workflow.py`; no page script; no out-of-date-move handling and no `tests/timed_run.py` change; the saved data format is unchanged; no confirmation on deleting a task or project; files other than the pages and routes that offer or confirm moves, and their tests, stay as they are; `pytest products/tracker17/tests/` keeps exiting 0.
**Language resolution:** Python — step 1, `project.md` `Languages:` — `core/doctrine/engineering/languages/python/README.md` opened and the concerns it routes read; no rule below renders a language-level fact

## Change Summary

After a move to `cancelled` is confirmed, going Back to the page that showed the confirmation brings that confirmation back today, live, because the browser restores the page from its history cache. After this change Back shows what the tracker's saved data says: the task is `cancelled`, there is no confirmation, and the refusal's reason describes the task's status. The same holds when another tab has moved the task on. No page gains a script. The change also records two review verdicts the previous change left open, its file fence and the browser tests' assertions, and lists the history mechanism it settles on as owed to a product record.

Paths below are relative to the product root, the directory `system/projects/tracker17/project.md` `Product path:` names. "The records" are that product's `docs/fdr.md`, `docs/SRS.md`, `docs/SDD.md`, `docs/nfdr.md`, `docs/UXD.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md`, `docs/CONTENT.md` and `docs/adr/`. "The confirmation's address" is the address a first press on `cancelled` lands on, on the task's page or on a project's page (today the one whose query carries `move_confirm=cancelled`). "The previous change" is `tracker17 · tobiishi · 2026-10-19_cancel-confirmation`, merged to `main` at `e0b0f245d` from base `e97b021f0`.

## Rules

### Going Back and Forward to a confirmation

- **CP-343:** Going Back or Forward in the browser to a confirmation's address, once the task's saved status no longer allows the move to `cancelled`, shows no confirmation. The page shows no sentence, no confirming control and no back-out control. It shows what a fresh load of that address shows for the saved status (previous change CP-331). The task's status reads as saved, and the task offers no move button. Keyboard focus is on the task's status in words, in the row or on the task's page. The move refusal's reason for that saved status is that element's accessible description. Nothing is saved: the saved data is byte-for-byte the same before and after the traversal (fdr R-102, R-106; SDD § 4 RT-7, "What a page shows is a function of the tracker state and the page's address"; SDD § 4 EX-6; UXD UX-F4, the second way a move refusal arises). It holds however the saved status came to disallow the move: by this tab's confirming press, or by moves made in another tab. It holds on the task's page and on a project's page at the task's row. The present defect: with the browser's history cache available, Back after confirming restores the old confirmation, strip and controls included. `tests/e2e/test_moves.py:423` fails today with `Locator expected to have count '0'`, `Actual value: 1` (Verified Claims).
- **CP-344:** Before the move is confirmed, reload, Back and Forward around a page showing a confirmation save nothing. The saved data is byte-for-byte unchanged (fdr R-106; SDD § 4 RT-8). While the task's saved status still allows the move, returning to the confirmation by reload or by Forward shows the confirmation with keyboard focus on its sentence (UXD UX-I2; previous change CP-326). That is what the history test asserts and passes today, up to line 423.
- **CP-345:** No page runs a script, and nothing in this change adds one (ADR-0003). No page gains a `script` element, an event-handler attribute or a script URL. The page policy still refuses every script on every page (NFDR QC-34). CP-343 and CP-344 are met by what the tracker sends, and by nothing a page runs. A test may run script inside the browser to observe it, as the history test's `pageshow` recorder does (`tests/e2e/test_moves.py:400-404`). That code is the test's own and is never part of a page the tracker serves.
- **CP-346:** CP-343 is not met by `Clear-Site-Data: "cache"`. Today every successful move response sends it (`web/routes.py:73`, `:743`), and on the pinned browser it does not evict the confirmation from the history cache (Verified Claims). Whether a response still carries it after this change is development-design's call, made under NFDR QC-12's review: does it buy anything a requirement pays for? If it stays, CP-350 names it with what it is for.

  Each case asserts the saved data byte-for-byte. `tests/e2e/test_moves.py::test_real_history_traversal_revalidates_the_confirmation` is that proof today for the task's page, and it passes after this change.

## Out of Scope

- **The words of a refusal reached with no press.** CONTENT.md version 12, § Message patterns, Error, gives a refusal reached by a reload, Back or an address its own form: "doesn't allow a move to cancelled", never "the move you pressed", with no action and no reload (CONTENT.md RN-26; UXD UX-I3 and UX-F4, the second way a move refusal arises; UXD RN-33). Today every move refusal says "the move you pressed" (`web/pages.py:760`), including the one a fresh load of the confirmation's address shows. This change does not alter that page. It makes Back reach it instead of the cached confirmation, and the request asks for "the saved-status refusal" without asking for new words. CP-343 pins that the reason describes the status, and pins no words, so the change that adopts the new form contradicts no rule of this one. Handed on as a record gap in `deferred_decisions.md`.
- **Back to a confirmation while the saved status still allows the move.** After another tab moves the task from `todo` to `in-progress` only, the move to `cancelled` is still allowed. Whether Back then shows a restored confirmation or a fresh one, a confirming press is checked against the saved status (previous change CP-332). How fresh a page shown from history is, beyond CP-343, belongs to the out-of-date-move work (`tracker17 · nemawashi · 2026-10-07_status-moves`, item 3, `out-of-date-move`).
- **Other pages restored from history.** A task's page or a project's page from before the first press may come back from the history cache with buttons the saved status no longer allows. A press on one is refused against the saved status (fdr R-102). Out-of-date pages and their refusal are `out-of-date-move`'s.

- **Writing the record.** No ADR is written and no line of `docs/SDD.md` or any other record changes here. CP-351 lists what is owed, for the record's owner.
- **The timed run.** `tests/timed_run.py` does not change, and this change adds no action SRS NFR-1 lists.
- **Confirmations for deleting a task or a project.** Already handed on by the previous change. This change adds no delete control.
- **A screen-reader pass.** No record requires it of this change (SDD § Open Questions, the second item).
- **The browser pins.** `tests/requirements.txt` and the browser it installs do not change (CP-347). Moving to another browser build to make the case pass is not this change.
