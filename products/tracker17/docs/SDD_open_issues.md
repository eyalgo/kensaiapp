### 5.1 The project-summary slice delivers no due-today count, and PRD version 9, SRS version 8 and UXD version 12 require one
- **Raised by:** Principal PM — 2026-11-13
- **Tracked by:** `tracker17 · nemawashi · 2026-11-13_the-sdd-carries-the-due-today-count`
- **What it blocks:** the seed that builds the count in the product. Cutting it from the PRD, the SRS or the UXD would double-cut it, and `products/tracker17/docs/what_left.md` names the count as not built. The FDR and the NFDR follow the SDD.
- **Where it shows:** § 6 S-6 `project-summary`, *Reached when* → *Behaviour*, lists "the total, a count for each status and each priority with zeros shown, the overdue count, the count with no due date, and the open tasks for each assignee" and names no due-today count; its *Traces to* stops at SRS FR-25, FR-38 and UXD UX-F6, UX-S4, UX-S5 without the count. SRS § 3.11 FR-38 now reads "beside the overdue count, the due-today count", and UXD § 5.6 UX-F6 and § 4 UX-S5 place it "directly after the overdue count as a plain number" that shows 0 (UXD version 12, `trail/uxd/cycle-10.md` § 3.1). `grep -ciE 'due.today'` over `SDD.md` finds none (run from `products/tracker17`, 2026-11-13). `S-6` is already built and retired, so open for the CTO to settle: whether the count is a new slice after `project-summary` or a re-cut of S-6 (§ 10 holds the earlier re-cuts, RC-1 to RC-6), what § 7 edge it takes, and what the layer-5 pass of § 4 must hold for a count of today's tasks that excludes `done` and `cancelled`.
- **If a document has to be written:** none. The SDD is revised by `product-sdd`.
- **Answer:** A re-cut of S-6, recorded as RC-7 in § 10, and no ninth slice. **Why a re-cut and not a slice:** the count is one more row of the summary `project-summary` already delivers, "where the project stands" (S-6 *Delivers*, unchanged); a slice made of one plain number alone is the fragment § 5 step 2 strikes and the "too narrow" cut of step 4, as RC-3 ruled for FR-41's paragraph in `first-project`. S-6's bar reads unmet on the day of the fold, and meeting it is the build's: the Principal PM cuts the build seed from S-6's unmet share (§ 8, *Reached when* divides). **Whose ground moved:** the CPO's, as the owner of the PRD, which changed upstream at version 9 on the owner's answer (`trail/prd/cycle-7.md` § 1.1), carried down by the SRS at version 8 (FR-38, AC-13, RN-26) and the UXD at version 12 (UX-F6, UX-S5, RN-38); one record covers the three, as RC-3 covered two. **S-6 after the re-cut:** *Behaviour* lists, directly after the overdue count, the due-today count — the project's tasks whose due date is today and whose status is neither `done` nor `cancelled` — and names it among the plain numbers with the no-due-date count, the per-assignee counts and "(unassigned)"; one summary uses one "today" for the overdue count and the due-today count, so a task due today is counted as due today and never as overdue; an empty project shows it as 0 with the rest (UX-F6, UX-S5; SRS FR-38). *Command* adds AC-13's due-today side: with today's date set by the test, an open task due today is in the due-today count and not overdue, and a task due today in `done` or `cancelled` is in neither count; AC-14's summary test covers the count unchanged. *Not in this slice* gains: a "Due today" marker on list rows and a due-today filter, which the PRD places in the summary only (SRS RN-26; UXD UX-F6) and no slice owns. *Artifact*, *Rests on*, *Moves toward* and *Why here in the order* stand, and *Traces to* already names FR-25, FR-38, UX-F6, UX-S5 and PRD § Core Features Project summary, which now state the count. The count's words on the page are `products/tracker17/docs/CONTENT.md`'s, "Due on {date}" (CONTENT version 22, RN-36), under § 4 EX-11; this document keeps the definition documents' phrase, "the due-today count". **§ 7:** no edge moves and none is added. `project-summary depends_on narrow-and-sort` rests on the linked counts, and the due-today count is a plain number, so it adds no dependency; what it needs, tasks with a due date and a status, `narrow-and-sort` already brings in through `tasks-in-a-project` and `status-moves`. **§ 4, the fifth group, Tests and the trace:** holds as written, with no condition added or changed. TS-3's `summary` row keeps FR-38, which now states the count, so TS-4's trace test reads the same word; TS-2 is how AC-13's due-today side sets the date, the clock handed in; TS-5's timed run already covers opening a summary, and the count rides the same read; TS-6's fake clock already supplies a date. The one-"today" rule is FR-38's and lands in S-6's behaviour line, not in § 4, because it binds the summary and not every slice; § 3 as written is how it holds: `application` reads the clock once per read and hands one date across `SM-2`, so the two counts cannot be worked out on different days. The rule is `domain`'s by § 3's table ("the overdue rule, the filter, sort and summary computations"), and how `domain/summary.py` works it out is the delivery Architect's (§ 12).
