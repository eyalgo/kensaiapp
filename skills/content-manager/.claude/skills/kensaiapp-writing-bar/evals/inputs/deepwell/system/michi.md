# 道 Michi — deepwell's Way(s)

*The instance-level declaration: the endless guiding goal(s) this instance commits to and never
finishes.*

---

## Way 1 — A stranger re-runs our results from the package alone  (slug: `rerun-from-the-package`)

Everything needed to get our numbers again is in the deposit, and nothing needed is outside it. The
test is a person we have never met, working from the download, with nobody to ask. If they have to
guess what we meant, the package has failed — not the reader.

## Way 2 — The package outlives the people who built it  (slug: `outlives-us`)

The deposit is read years after the last author has left. Nothing in it may depend on knowing who
wrote it, what was obvious that season, or what was said in a meeting. What is not written down is
gone.
