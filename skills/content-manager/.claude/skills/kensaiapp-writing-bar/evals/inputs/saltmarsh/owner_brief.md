# Note from the owner — 2026-08-14

I am not convinced we need much written down about how we write. Nothing we produce here goes to a
stranger — it is eleven engineers reading each other's runbooks, and they have managed so far.

There is one rule I do want: **every document that states a time states its timezone in the same
breath.** Three services, a rota across two continents, and last month a runbook that said "restart
at 02:00" cost us forty minutes and a bad-tempered handover.

I do not want this only for us. Every instance has this problem the moment two people are in
different places, and I would rather it were written down once, wherever rules like that live, than
copied into each instance separately and drifting.

Put it wherever it actually belongs, and tell me where that is.
