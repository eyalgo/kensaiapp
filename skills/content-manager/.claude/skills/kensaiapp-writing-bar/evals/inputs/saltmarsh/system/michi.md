# 道 Michi — saltmarsh's Way(s)

*The instance-level declaration: the endless guiding goal(s) this instance commits to and never
finishes.*

---

## Way 1 — On-call never needs a second person  (slug: `alone-at-3am`)

Whoever holds the pager can finish the job alone. Every runbook step is one the person on duty can
take without waking someone else up, and anything that genuinely needs two people says so at the top
rather than three steps in.

## Way 2 — The handover loses nothing  (slug: `clean-handover`)

Work passes between continents twice a day. What one shift knows, the next shift reads — no state
lives only in someone's head, and no thread ends with "ask me tomorrow".
