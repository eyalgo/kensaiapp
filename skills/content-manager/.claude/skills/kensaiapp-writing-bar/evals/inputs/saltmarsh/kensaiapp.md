# saltmarsh — Claude Code instance

## What this is about

This is **saltmarsh** — the instance that carries the three internal services the company's field
teams depend on: the shift roster, the parts catalogue, and the dispatch queue.

The work here is operational. saltmarsh holds the runbooks, the on-call rota, and the change record
for all three services. Its readers are the engineers on that rota — eleven of them, spread across
two continents, handing work to each other twice a day.

Nothing here is published outside the company.

## Prime directives

None beyond the user-global rules.
