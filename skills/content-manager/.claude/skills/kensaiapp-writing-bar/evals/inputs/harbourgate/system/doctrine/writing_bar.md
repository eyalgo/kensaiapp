## 0. Where this sits

This is the bar for every document kensaiapp authors in northbay. What a writing bar reaches, and
what it does not, belongs to `prompts/rich.md`.

## 1. The bar

**Write it the way you would say it to the person sitting next to you.** A document here is read by
someone on the team, and it should sound like one of them talking.

- **Say the conclusion first, then why.** A reader who stops after the first line should still have
  the answer.
- **No shorthand.** If a phrase would need explaining to a new joiner, write the longer plain
  version instead.
- **Name a script or a setting and say what it does in the same breath.** The name on its own tells
  a reader nothing.
- **Two short sentences beat one long one.** Length is not the problem; stacked clauses are.
