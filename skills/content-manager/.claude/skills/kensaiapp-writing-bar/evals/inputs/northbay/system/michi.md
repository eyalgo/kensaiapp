# 道 Michi — northbay's Way(s)

*The instance-level declaration: the endless guiding goal(s) this instance commits to and never
finishes.*

---

## Way 1 — One person can hold the whole release  (slug: `one-head`)

A release is cut by one person in one sitting. Anything that needs two people in the room, or a
conversation before it can start, is a defect in the tooling rather than a fact about releases. The
checklist is short enough to read before the coffee goes cold.

## Way 2 — Every automatic step has a hand-run twin  (slug: `hand-switchable`)

Nothing here runs only as automation. Each scheduled job has a documented way to do the same thing by
hand, so the team is never waiting on a green tick it cannot reproduce.
