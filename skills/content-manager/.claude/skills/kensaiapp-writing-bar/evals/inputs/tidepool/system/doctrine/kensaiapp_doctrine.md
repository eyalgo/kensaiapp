# tidepool — domain doctrine

*The instance's **general domain doctrine**: the standing rules, vocabulary, and invariants of this
instance's subject area — what every project here inherits.*

---

> **STUB — populate as the instance's domain takes shape.** A fresh instance has no domain rules yet. Add
> the standing vocabulary, invariants, and cross-project rules of this instance's subject area here as
> they emerge. Rules specific to a single product belong in that project's domain doctrine
> (`system/projects/<name>/doctrine/<project>_doctrine.md`), not here.
