# PRD — Briefbox

**Status:** Settled
**Owner:** CPO
**Version:** 1 — first authoring settled everything from the product request; no companion was needed.

> Briefbox is a personal reading digest: subscribers get one calm, curated email a week. This PRD
> covers the public signup surface — the only surface v1 has.

---

## 1. Problem

Busy professionals subscribe to a dozen newsletters and read none of them. Briefbox promises the
opposite trade: one email a week, five links, each with a two-sentence reason it's worth the time.

## 2. Target users

Mid-career professionals who read on phones, in the evening, tired. They arrive at the signup
page from a shared link and decide in under thirty seconds.

## 3. Goals

- **G1 — Convert a cold visit.** A visitor who lands understands the promise and can subscribe in
  one step.
- **G2 — Feel calm.** The page itself must embody the product's promise: quiet, unhurried,
  confidence without noise.

## 4. Features

- **F1 — The pitch.** One screen stating the promise, a sample issue excerpt, and social proof
  (subscriber count).
- **F2 — Signup.** A single email field with inline validation and a clear confirmation state.
- **F3 — Sample issue.** A link to one full past issue, rendered on the same surface.

## 5. Scope

v1 is **one public page** — its pitch, its signup and confirmation states, and one full past
issue rendered inline on that same page. There is no second surface.
No app, no account area, no dashboard. The weekly email itself is written and sent through an
external service and is out of this product's scope.

## 6. UX Notes

The page should read like the product: generous whitespace, an editorial voice, one action. The
confirmation state should feel like a welcome, not a receipt.
