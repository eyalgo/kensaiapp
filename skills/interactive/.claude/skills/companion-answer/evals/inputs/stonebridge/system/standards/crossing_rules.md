# Crossing rules — Stonebridge

**Status:** Draft

How the Stonebridge centre span is worked. Every measured figure below is cited from
`system/standards/river_survey.md` and is not restated as a fact of its own.

## 1. Scope

These rules bind every powered vessel transiting the centre span, and every unpowered vessel under tow.
A vessel that turns back before the span is outside them.

## 2. Air draught

A vessel of more than **4.2 m** air draught may not transit the centre span. The figure is the survey's
headroom at that span (`river_survey.md` § 3).

The flanking spans are not available as an alternative: they give less headroom on the same measurement,
and neither carries a marked channel.

## 3. Transit signalling

A vessel calls the crossing on VHF 12 before entering the reach, and again on clearing the span. The
crossing keeper records both calls in the transit log.

## 4. Enforcement

A transit above the § 2 limit is recorded against the vessel and reported to the navigation authority.
Three recorded transits in a rolling year suspend the vessel's right of passage for six months.

## 5. Weather

The span closes to all traffic when the observed wind at the crossing exceeds 25 knots, or when
visibility at the keeper's station falls below 200 m.
