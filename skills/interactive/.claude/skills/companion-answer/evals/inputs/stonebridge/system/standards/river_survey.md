# River survey — Stonebridge reach

**Status:** Settled

The measured description of the Stonebridge reach. Every figure the crossing's operating documents cite
is fixed here and nowhere else, so that a number moves in one place when a resurvey moves it.

## 1. Extent

The reach runs from the tidal limit at Aylmer Weir to the confluence at Stone Point, 4.6 km. The centre
span of Stonebridge sits at 2.1 km from the weir.

## 2. Channel

The navigable channel is 31 m wide at the centre span, narrowing to 24 m at the Stone Point bend. The
bed is gravel over clay throughout; two scour holes are recorded, at 1.4 km and at 2.0 km.

## 3. Headroom at the centre span

**4.2 m.** Measured 2024-04-11 from the underside of the centre span's lowest structural member to the
water surface at the time of survey.

The two flanking spans give 3.6 m and 3.7 m on the same measurement. The reach carries no other fixed
structure over the channel.

## 4. Flow

Mean flow at the centre span is 0.7 m/s. The reach is tidal to Aylmer Weir; the observed range at the
centre span is 1.8 m between mean high water and mean low water springs.

## 5. Resurvey

The reach is resurveyed every three years, or after any recorded scour event at the centre span. The
next scheduled resurvey is 2027-04.
