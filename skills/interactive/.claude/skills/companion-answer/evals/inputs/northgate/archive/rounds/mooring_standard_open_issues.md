## 1. Open issues

### 1.1 How often is a mooring lifted for inspection?
- **Why it matters:** § 2 states the interval, and § 4's record retention counts from it.
- **Answer:** Annually, in the six weeks after the equinox — that is when the pool is emptiest and the
  lift boat is already on charter for the pontoon survey.
