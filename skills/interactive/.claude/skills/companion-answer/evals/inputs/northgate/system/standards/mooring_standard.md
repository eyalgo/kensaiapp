# Mooring standard — Northgate

**Status:** Settled

## 1. What this standard covers

Ground tackle, riser and pennant specification for every swinging mooring in the Northgate pool.

## 2. Inspection interval

Every mooring is lifted and inspected annually, in the six weeks after the equinox.

## 3. Load rating

Each mooring carries a plated rating. A vessel above the plated displacement is refused the mooring.

## 4. Records

The lift record is held for seven years and is produced on request to the insurer.
