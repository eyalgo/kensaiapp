# Ledger standard — Ledgerworks

**Round:** 2
**Status:** Draft

## 1. Purpose

How a movement is recorded, corrected and closed in the Ledgerworks books.

## 2. Correcting a posted entry

A posted entry is never edited. A correcting entry is posted against it, carrying the original's
reference and the reason for the correction.

## 3. Period close

A period closes when every account reconciles and the Registrar signs the close. After the signature no
entry may be posted into that period.

## 4. Retention

Closed periods are retained for the statutory term.
