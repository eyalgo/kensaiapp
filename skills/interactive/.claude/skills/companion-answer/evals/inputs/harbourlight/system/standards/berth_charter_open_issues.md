- **Why it matters:** § 3 of the charter sets a maximum idle period, and every enforcement line below it

  `system/standards/`, and the port authority's published tariff. The bylaw defers to "the harbour's own
  posted limit", the agreements name no period, and the tariff prices by day without capping the stay.

## 2. Choices to confirm

### 2.1 Overnight arrivals are logged by the duty officer, not self-declared
- **Written into the charter:** "A vessel arriving between 20:00 and 06:00 is logged by the duty officer
  at the time it makes fast; the master's own declaration is recorded beside it, not in place of it."
- **Grounds:** the two disputed arrivals in the 2024 incident log both turned on a self-declared time,
  and both were resolved against the declaring master. No rule states this, so it is the Harbourmaster's
  call, made to keep § 4 enforceable.
- **If overridden:** § 4's dispute procedure loses the record it resolves against, and the penalty table
  in § 5 has nothing to key an overnight surcharge to.
