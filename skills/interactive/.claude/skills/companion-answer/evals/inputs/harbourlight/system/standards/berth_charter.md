# Berth charter — Harbourlight

**Status:** Draft — open issues outstanding

## 1. What this charter covers

The allocation, occupation and release of every berth inside the inner harbour wall.

## 2. Scope

Vessels of 5 m length overall and above. Craft below that length are handled by the tender rules and
are outside this charter.

## 3. Idle period

A vessel may hold an allocated berth without moving for no longer than the posted limit. The limit is
not yet fixed; § 3.1 through § 3.4 count from it and are drafted against a placeholder.

## 4. Arrival logging

A vessel arriving between 20:00 and 06:00 is logged by the duty officer at the time it makes fast; the
master's own declaration is recorded beside it, not in place of it. A dispute is resolved against the
duty officer's log.

## 5. Penalties

Keyed to the idle period in § 3 and to the arrival record in § 4.

## 6. Tenders

A tender under 5 m is exempt from the idle limit in § 3.
