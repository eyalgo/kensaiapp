# 根回し Nemawashi seed — tides from a second source when the authority's feed is late

## What it is

When the harbour authority's feed is late, no tides are computed and no alert leaves. A second feed
would cover the gap. The authority has said it will publish a mirror feed; it has not, and no other
source is licensed for these harbours, so nothing here can be built until the mirror exists.

## Source

The harbour authority's notice of a planned mirror feed, forwarded by the owner. It gives no date.

## Out (later / elsewhere)

- Buying a commercial feed — ruled out by the owner on cost.
