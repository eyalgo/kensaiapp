# SRS — brackenway

**Status:** Settled · **Version:** 3

## Functional requirements

### FR-1 — Trail map
Every trail in the park's survey file is drawn on the map.

### FR-2 — Closures
A ranger posts or lifts a closure on a named trail from the ranger page.

### FR-3 — Freshness
A posted or lifted closure shows on the map within 15 minutes.

### FR-4 — Nearest open trail
A walker who shares their location sees the nearest trail with no closure on it.
