# 根回し Nemawashi seed — a shop gets one invoice a week

## What it is

Shops are charged per booking today and asked for one invoice a week instead. Done: each Monday a shop
gets one invoice for last week's fees.

## Source

Three shops asked the owner for it; the owner forwarded their emails.

## Stations — dynamic checklist

- [ ] invoice-is-drawn-up — last week's fees become one invoice
- [ ] invoice-is-sent — the invoice reaches the shop each Monday
