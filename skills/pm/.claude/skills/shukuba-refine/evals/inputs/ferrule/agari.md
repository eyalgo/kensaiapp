# 上がり Agari — ferrule's "done"

ferrule lets a small shop book a courier pickup, see where it stands, and cancel it.

**Reached when** all hold:

1. A shop books a pickup in under a minute, and the courier gets it.
2. A shop sees each pickup's status as the courier updates it.
3. A shop cancels a pickup and is charged exactly what the SRS says a cancellation costs.
